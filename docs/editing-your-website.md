# Editing your website

Your website has two parts. The **website** is what visitors see. The **editor** is where you change
what it says. You log into the editor, change some words or add a date, press Publish, and about a
minute later the website updates. You never need to touch the website itself.

## Logging in

Go to **https://origine-healing.sanity.studio** and sign in with your Google account or email.
Save that address somewhere. It works on your phone too, though a laptop is easier for longer text.

## What you will see

Down the left side there is a list:

- **Pages**: the fixed pages (Home, About, Work with me, and so on). Each one has its headline and
  introduction, and for Home and About, all of the text on that page.
- **Offers and prices**: everything people can book. A single session, the three-month programme,
  Moonlight Meditation, retreats. Prices live here and nowhere else.
- **Events and dates**: the next Moonlight Meditation, a workshop, a retreat with dates. Add one here
  and it appears on the Groups and retreats page and on the home page. Past dates disappear by themselves.
- **Modalities**: Access Bars, Reiki, and the rest, each with its explanation.
- **Blog posts**
- **FAQ questions**
- **Client quotes**: the testimonials.
- **Contact details and links**: your email, WhatsApp number, Calendly link, social links, your
  reply promise and cancellation policy.

Click anything to open it. The fields are labelled in plain words, and most have a short note under
them saying what they are for.

## Making a change

1. Open the thing you want to change.
2. Edit the text. Changes save automatically as you type, as a draft. Nobody can see a draft.
3. When you are happy, press the green **Publish** button at the bottom.
4. Wait about a minute, then look at the website. If you do not see the change, refresh the page.

If you change your mind before publishing, open the menu next to Publish and choose **Discard changes**.

## Common jobs

**Change a price.** Offers and prices → open the offer → change the number under Price → Publish.
The price updates on every page and button at once.

**Add the next Moonlight Meditation.** Events and dates → the plus button → give it a name like
"Moonlight Meditation, November" → under "Which offer is this a date for?" choose Moonlight Meditation
→ set Starts and Ends → Publish. It will show on the home page as the next date.

**Post a retreat.** Same as above, choosing the Retreats offer. If the price for this retreat is
different, fill in "Price for this date". Write what is special about this one in "About this date".

**Write a blog post.** Blog posts → plus → Title, Date, Summary (one or two sentences) → Cover photo →
write in "The post". The text box works like a simple word processor: highlight words for bold or
italic, use the dropdown for headings, and the plus in the margin to add a photo. Publish when done.

**Add a client quote.** Client quotes → plus → paste what they said, add the name they agreed to.
Turn on "Show on the home page" if you want it there. Two are shown on the home page at a time.

**Change your email, WhatsApp or Calendly link.** Contact details and links → edit → Publish.

**Add a photo.** Wherever there is a photo field, click it and choose a file from your computer.
Every photo asks for a short description. Write what is in the picture, one sentence. It is read
aloud to blind visitors and read by Google, so it matters.

## Things that are safe to ignore

- The **French version** section on each item is folded up. Leave it alone until the French site is
  ready. Nothing breaks if it is empty.
- The **Search and sharing** section on each page. The site fills this in for you. Only change it if
  someone who knows about Google asks you to.
- The **Vision** tab at the top. It is a tool for developers.

## If something goes wrong

- The website shows old text: wait two minutes and refresh. If it is still old, check the item is
  Published, not just saved as a draft (the Publish button is grey when there is nothing to publish).
- You deleted something by mistake: open the item, open the menu next to Publish and choose
  **Review changes** or **Restore** to go back to an earlier version.
- You cannot log in: use the "forgot password" link, or ask Caellum to send a new invite.

Anything else, message Caellum. Nothing you can do in the editor can break the website.
