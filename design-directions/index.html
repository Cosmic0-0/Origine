Three homepage directions

Same copy, same photographs, same five brand colours in each. Open on a phone as well as a laptop; the phone layout is the one that matters most.

  1. A. MarginaliaWhite page, book-like. A narrow margin column carries running notes (prices, place, dates) beside a wide reading column. Cormorant Garamond at large sizes. The quietest of the three.
  2. B. EveningThe site lives on the slate teal with sand type, like the indoor photograph. Fraunces display. One terracotta moon is the only shape on the page. The boldest.
  3. C. ShorelinePale as the beach sky, photo-forward. A single terracotta horizon line runs through the page and images break above and below it. Fraunces at a softer weight.

Photography by Sita Kelly Photography. Fonts load from Google Fonts in these mock-ups only; the built site self-hosts them.
