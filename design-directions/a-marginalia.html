OrigineFrench for origin. A return to your natural state of being.

Healing is not about fixing what is broken. It is about returning to what is already whole within you.

I trained in sports science, psychology and occupational therapy, and taught clinical and rehabilitative Pilates for more than twenty years. Then I went further, into hypnotherapy, embodiment coaching and shamanic practice. Origine is where the two halves meet: a practice that takes your body as seriously as your story.

Chronic pain. Anxiety. A life in transition.
